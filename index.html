muggzy™

The party mug that hangs on you.

Muggzy is a light insulated mug with a lid and a swappable strap. Wear it across your chest, let go, and it hangs upright, so your drink stays with you and both hands stay free. Made for graduations, Greek life, bachelorette parties, and tailgates.

What makes it different.

A middle ground between a light aluminum mug and a heavy insulated tumbler, built to be worn.

Hangs straight, hands free

The strap clips to two opposite points above the mug's center of gravity, so it hangs upright like a bucket the moment you let go.

Lighter than a tumbler, colder than aluminum

Insulated for a 3 hour party, and light enough to wear all night. We are aiming for 300 g or less.

Your drink stays with you

A light lid snaps shut and opens with one hand. It cuts spills when you dance and helps make tampering harder.

Straps that swap in seconds

Change colors for every event in about 10 seconds. The exclusive clip only fits Muggzy straps.

Made for your group

Custom straps with your name, crest, or colors, plus collabs with frats, sororities, and student orgs.

Drink from a real mug

Skip the bottle. Pour what you want and sip from a mug that goes wherever you go.

How it works.

Three moves, no tools.

Step 1

Clip it on

The strap locks into two opposite points above the mug's center of gravity.

Step 2

Let go

The mug swings, then hangs straight, like a bucket on a handle. Both hands are free.

Step 3

Swap it

Change the strap in about 10 seconds with one hand. The clip only fits Muggzy straps.

Questions, answered.

How long will drinks stay cold?

Our v1 target is 10 °C or below after 3 hours, starting at 4 °C in a 30 °C room. We will publish real test results as prototypes come in.

Does it fit in a car cup holder?

That is the target: a base of 7.5 cm or smaller. We will test it in three different cars.

Can I put it in the dishwasher?

We are designing for 50 dishwasher cycles without peeling or fading, and we will confirm it in testing.

Does the lid stop people from spiking my drink?

No lid can promise that. It is designed to help make it harder for anyone to tamper with your drink, so keep an eye on your cup.
